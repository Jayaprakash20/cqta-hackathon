# Code of Conduct

This repository is a public collaboration space for the CQTA hackathon community.

## Our Standards
Participants are expected to:
- Be respectful and professional.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Credit others for their work.
- Keep discussions focused on collaboration and learning.

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private or sensitive information.
- Spam, malicious links, or harmful content.

## Enforcement
Organizers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and comments that violate these standards.
Repeated or severe violations may lead to repository access restrictions.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to:
- contact@cqtacanada.com
